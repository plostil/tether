A phone joins by scanning that QR with its camera. After the first pairing, this device is remembered — next time just tap "Reconnect", no QR needed.

This browser can't capture a screen (iPhone Safari has no screen-capture API), so this device can view the other screen but not share its own. To share a PC's screen, open this page in Chrome on the PC.

Live mouse/keyboard control of the other device isn't possible from a browser page and needs the native app (still in the works). For now you get live screen view plus the message box below.
